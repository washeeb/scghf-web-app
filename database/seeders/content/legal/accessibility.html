Our commitment

We want everybody to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, who navigate by keyboard, who need larger text, or who are on a slow connection or an older phone. We aim to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2.

What we have done

  • Every page works without JavaScript, including giving, buying and registering. Nothing depends on a script that may not load.
  • Every page has one main heading, a sensible heading order, and a "skip to content" link for keyboard users.
  • Every form field has a visible label; errors are announced and tied to the field they belong to.
  • Every image published on the site carries a description; a photograph without one is not published.
  • Text and background colours meet the AA contrast ratio in both the light and the dark theme, and the theme follows your device's setting unless you choose otherwise.
  • Pages are built to load quickly on a 3G connection, with images sized for the screen they are shown on.
  • Animation respects your device's "reduce motion" setting.

How we checked

In September 2026 every kind of public page was tested with the axe accessibility engine against WCAG 2.2 level AA in both the light and the dark theme, and by hand with a keyboard. Two problems were found and fixed: a link in the footer that was too small to tap comfortably, and headings on the projects, appeals and questions pages that skipped a level. An automated check of every public page now runs every time the site is changed, so those problems cannot come back unnoticed.

What may not work well

Documents on our reports page are PDFs supplied to us and may not all be accessible. If you need the content of one in another form, ask us and we will provide it.

Events

Each event page describes the physical access at the venue in words — step-free entrance, accessible toilet, parking — rather than with a symbol, so you can decide whether it works for you. The registration form asks what we can do to make the day accessible for you, and only the organisers see the answer.

Tell us

If you find something on this site you cannot use, please tell us which page and what happened. We will fix what we can and tell you when we have.
