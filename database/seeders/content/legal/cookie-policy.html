What a cookie is

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to remember. We use a handful, all of them to make the site work or to remember a choice you made. We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not use any third-party analytics that track you across other sites.

Cookies set by others

When you pay, you are taken to Paystack's payment page, which sets its own cookies under its own policy. When you come back to us, none of them come with you.

Visitor counting

We count how many people visit each page so we know what is read. The count does not use a cookie and does not record who you are, your address or your device.

Controlling cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser's settings. If you block the session cookie, forms — including the donation form — will stop working; the rest of the site will still be readable.
