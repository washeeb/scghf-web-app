Our position

Money given to us is given in trust. We do not tolerate fraud, theft, bribery or the misuse of the foundation's money, goods or name by anybody — trustee, staff, volunteer, partner or supplier — and we will report it to the police.

How we prevent it

  • Every payment into the foundation is recorded against a reference, reconciled against the payment provider's records, and reported. Money cannot be marked as received by a person: it is confirmed by the payment provider directly.
  • Every payment out is recorded with who was paid, for what, from which appeal, and who approved it, and appeals publish what was paid out beside what was raised.
  • Access to financial records is limited by role, every staff account requires a second factor to sign in, and changes to financial records are logged in a tamper-evident audit trail.
  • Stock in our shop is a ledger: every movement has a reason and a name, and an adjustment with no explanation is treated as a loss.
  • The accounts are prepared annually and made available on our reports page.

Fraud against you

People sometimes use a charity's name to ask for money that never reaches it. We will never ask you for a donation by telephone or text message out of the blue, never ask for your card details by email, and never send somebody to your door to collect cash. Our only online donation page is on this website, and our bank and Mobile Money details are published on our other ways to give page and nowhere else. If you are asked for money in our name in any other way, please tell us.

Card and payment fraud

Our donation and checkout pages are protected against automated attempts to test stolen card details. A gift that appears to be such an attempt is refunded and reported. If you see a payment to us on your statement that you did not make, contact your bank and then us, quoting the reference.

Reporting

If you suspect fraud in connection with our work, raise it through our raising a concern page. You may do so anonymously, and you will be protected for doing so in good faith.
