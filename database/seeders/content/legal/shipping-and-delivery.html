Draft for the trustees to review. Not published. The charges and times below are shown to customers from the delivery zones set up in the admin; this page explains the arrangement rather than repeating the numbers.

Where we deliver

We deliver to the regions listed at checkout. The charge depends on the region and, for heavy orders, on the weight; it is shown region by region on the checkout page before you choose, and the exact total is shown on the payment page before you pay. Some orders qualify for free delivery above a certain value, and the checkout says when yours does.

If your region is not listed, we cannot deliver there at the moment. You may be able to collect instead, or you can contact us and we will see what can be arranged.

Collection

You can choose to collect your order from us free of charge. Where and when to collect is shown at checkout, and we will let you know when it is ready.

How long it takes

Each delivery rate shows an estimated time at checkout. It starts from when your payment is confirmed, not from when you placed the order. We will email you when your order has been dispatched, with the courier's name and a tracking reference where there is one, and we will send a text message to the phone number on the order.

Your address

Please give us the region, the town or area, and enough for a courier to find you — a street, a landmark, a GhanaPost GPS address. The phone number on the order is the one the courier will call. If we cannot reach you and the parcel is returned to us, we will contact you to arrange redelivery; a second delivery charge may apply.

Downloads

A download is not posted. Once your payment is confirmed, a link arrives by email. It works a limited number of times and for a limited period, both stated in the email.

Problems

If an order arrives damaged, is missing something, or does not arrive within the estimated time, tell us through the contact page quoting your order reference. Our refund policy says what we will do.
