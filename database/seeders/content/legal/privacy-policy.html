Who we are

We are a registered Ghanaian non-profit foundation. In this policy "we" and "the foundation" mean the organisation named at the foot of every page. We are a data controller under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and we are registered, or are registering, with the Data Protection Commission as the Act requires.

What we collect, and why

We collect only what we need for a purpose we can name.

  • When you donate: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, the amount and the appeal you chose, and the payment reference. We need these to take the payment, to issue your receipt, and to keep the financial records the law requires. We never see or store your card or Mobile Money details; they go directly to our payment provider, Paystack.
  • When you buy from the shop: your name, email, phone number and delivery address, and what you ordered. We need these to take the payment, to deliver the order and to invoice it.
  • When you register for an event: your name, email, phone number if you give it, how many people you are bringing, and anything you tell us about access or dietary needs. Access and dietary needs can reveal health information; we ask for them only so we can look after you on the day, and only the organisers see them.
  • When you apply to volunteer: your contact details, your date of birth where a role requires a police clearance, what you tell us about yourself, your next of kin if you give one, and any disclosure you make. Because our work involves children and vulnerable adults, we carry out safeguarding checks, and we keep a record of them.
  • When you write to us: your name, email, phone number if you give it, and your message.
  • When you subscribe to our newsletter: your email address, and the record that you confirmed you wanted it.
  • When you create an account: your name, email address and a password, which is stored only in a form we cannot read back.

The legal basis

For a donation, an order or an event registration, we process your details because that is what you asked us to do. For safeguarding checks on volunteers, we process them because we have a duty to protect the people we serve. For the newsletter and for any other message about our work that is not a receipt or a confirmation, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the link in every such email. For financial records, we keep what the law requires us to keep.

Beneficiaries and photographs

The people we serve are not the subject of this website's forms, but they are the subject of our work. We do not publish a photograph of a child or a vulnerable adult without recorded consent from them or from the person responsible for them, and we remove location data from every photograph before it is published. Where we publish numbers about the people we help, we withhold any figure small enough to identify somebody.

Who we share it with

  • Paystack, which takes payments on our behalf. Paystack sees your payment details; we see only whether the payment succeeded, a reference, and the fee.
  • Our email and SMS providers, which deliver receipts, confirmations and, if you have consented, updates.
  • A courier, if you order something for delivery: your name, address and phone number, and nothing else.
  • Our web host, which stores the website and its database.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anybody else except where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep financial records, including donation and order records, for at least six years, because the law requires it. We keep contact enquiries, event registrations and communication logs for two years. We keep a declined or withdrawn volunteer application for six to twelve months, and a volunteer's record, including safeguarding checks, for six years after they leave, because an allegation made years later must be able to be investigated against what was known at the time. Abandoned shop baskets are deleted after thirty days. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under Act 843 you may ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we have no continuing duty to keep it, and object to its processing. To do any of these, write to us at the address at the foot of this page. We will answer within the time the Act allows. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection. Staff access to personal data is limited by role, every staff account requires a second factor to sign in, and access to sensitive records is logged. Documents about beneficiaries and volunteers are kept on private storage that is not reachable from the internet.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies, none of them for advertising. Our cookie policy lists them.

Changes
