Two points need a decision: whether the foundation holds a current tax-exemption approval from the Ghana Revenue Authority, which decides what a receipt may say; and the percentage, if any, that the trustees allow to be taken from a restricted gift towards running costs.

Where your gift goes

A gift to a named appeal is spent on that appeal. A gift with no appeal chosen, or made through the "wherever it is needed most" option, goes to our General Fund, which we spend on whichever part of our work needs it most at the time. We publish, for every appeal, what was raised and what has been paid out from it, so that the two can be compared.

When an appeal reaches its target

Each appeal says what happens when its target is reached: it may keep accepting gifts, which then extend or continue the same work; it may close; or it may direct further gifts to a related appeal. The appeal page always says which, and always says when the target has been reached, so nobody gives to a met goal without knowing it.

When an appeal cannot go ahead

If a project cannot proceed as described — because circumstances change, because a partner withdraws, because the need is met by somebody else — we will apply gifts to the nearest similar work and say so on the appeal page. Where a gift was large and specific, we will contact the donor first and offer to return it.

Costs

Running a foundation costs money: staff, transport, the fees charged on every payment. When you give online you are offered the choice to cover the payment provider's fee so that the appeal receives the whole amount; if you do not, the fee is deducted before the gift is applied. Beyond that, the trustees set the share of unrestricted income that may be used for running costs, and publish it in the annual report.

Receipts

Every online gift is acknowledged by email with a reference. Where the foundation holds a current approval from the Ghana Revenue Authority, the acknowledgement says so and may be used in support of a tax claim; where it does not, the acknowledgement says that too. A receipt is never issued for a shop purchase, because a purchase is not a gift.

Gifts we cannot accept

We will decline, or return, a gift that we have reason to believe is the proceeds of crime, that comes with conditions we cannot meet or that would compromise our independence, or that appears to be an attempt to test stolen payment details. Unusually large gifts made through the website may be held while we contact the donor.

Monthly giving

A monthly gift is set up only once the first payment has been confirmed. You can stop it at any time from your account or by contacting us, and we will confirm that it has stopped. If a monthly payment fails we will try again and let you know; we will not keep trying indefinitely.

Gifts in kind and offline gifts

Gifts of goods are covered on our donate goods page. Gifts made by bank transfer or Mobile Money are recorded and acknowledged when we can match them to a donor; please quote the reference we give you.
