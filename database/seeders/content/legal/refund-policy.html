Donations

A donation is a gift, and once made it is normally final. We will refund a donation in these cases:

  • It was made in error — a wrong amount, a duplicate payment, a gift made from the wrong account — and you tell us within fourteen days.
  • It was made without the authority of the account holder.
  • The appeal it was made to cannot go ahead and we cannot apply the gift to similar work in a way you are content with.

To ask for a refund, contact us with the reference from your acknowledgement. A refund is returned to the same card or Mobile Money account the gift came from, through our payment provider, and normally arrives within ten working days. Where a receipt has been issued that could support a tax claim, it is cancelled when the gift is refunded.

Shop orders

You may cancel an order at any time before it is dispatched, for a full refund. Once dispatched:

  • If an item arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, tell us within seven days of receiving it, with a photograph, and we will replace it or refund it in full, including the delivery charge.
  • If you change your mind, you may return an unused item in its original condition within fourteen days of receiving it. We will refund the price of the item; the cost of returning it is yours, and the original delivery charge is not refunded.
  • Downloads cannot be returned once the link has been used.

A refund is returned to the payment method used, through our payment provider. Stock is put back on sale when a refund is agreed.

Event registrations

Registration for our events is free, so there is nothing to refund. If you cannot come, please tell us so that your place can go to somebody on the waiting list.

How to ask

Write to us through the contact page, quoting the reference on your acknowledgement or order confirmation. We will acknowledge your request and tell you what will happen next.
