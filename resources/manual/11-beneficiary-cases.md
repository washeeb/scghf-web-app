# 11. Beneficiary cases

*Programmes → Beneficiary cases.* A case is one person the foundation has
been asked to help: what they asked for, what was decided, what was paid,
and the papers behind it. This is the most sensitive screen in the panel
and it behaves differently from every other one. Read this chapter before
you open a case.

## Who sees what

What you see on a case depends on who you are **to that case**, not only
on your job:

| You are… | You see |
|---|---|
| the **case worker** on it | everything, and you can edit and add notes |
| the **Safeguarding Lead** | everything on every case; you decide, reassign, record consent and export |
| a Programme Officer **not** on the case | the summary only: reference, status, name, district, who has it |
| **Finance** | approved cases only: the name, the reference, the amount and the account to pay it into |
| the **Auditor** | the process — dates, the application, the log, the money — never contact details, health or ID |
| the Super Admin | everything, read-only on the sensitive section |

The sensitive section — health, religion, the Ghana Card number, the
identity scan — is collapsed and shown only to the worker and the
Safeguarding Lead. **Every time anybody opens a case it is recorded**,
with the tier they saw. Revealing the Ghana Card number in full is
recorded separately, with your name, in the case log. Open a case
because you need to, not to look.

## A new case

**New case.** The first section is consent: the applicant — or, for a
child, a parent or guardian — signs the paper consent form and you upload
a photograph or scan of it. **The case cannot be saved without it.** A
child cannot consent for themselves; record the parent or guardian.

Fill in what you know. Only the name is required; the rest can be added
later. If the Ghana Card number matches an earlier case, the save is
refused and the earlier reference is shown — open it first. If it really
is a new application, tick **I have checked** and save again.

You become the case worker. The case starts as a **draft** that only you
and the Safeguarding Lead can see in full.

## The case's life

**Submit for review** (you) → **Start review** and **Approve** or
**Decline** (the Safeguarding Lead) → **Close case** (you or the Lead,
with an outcome) once the help has been given.

- **Approved** makes the case visible to Finance, who raise the payment
  under *Finance → Payouts* and link it to the case. What was paid shows
  under *Money paid* on the case.
- **Decline** asks why, for the file. What the applicant is told is a
  letter, written separately.
- **Withdraw** is for an application that will not be completed.
- **Close** asks for the outcome and starts the retention clock: the
  record is kept six years, medical documents two, and then removed
  automatically. **Nothing is deleted today, and there is no delete
  button** — closing is the end of a case.

## Notes, documents, consent

**Add a note** writes a dated entry with your name. It cannot be edited or
removed afterwards, so write what you would be content for the person to
read. Every action on the case also writes a line in the log.

**Documents** — *Add a document*: a PDF or a photograph, with a type.
Medical and identity documents are sensitive: only the worker and the
Safeguarding Lead can open them, and they are removed two years after the
case closes. **Open** gives you a link that works for five minutes; do not
paste it into WhatsApp — it will be dead before it is read, and the
opening was recorded against your name.

**Consent** — the data-processing consent from intake is there. A
photograph, a story or the person's name on the website each need their
**own** consent row, recorded here with the signed form under Documents.
No row means no publication; the media library enforces it. Revoke with a
reason and the pictures stop appearing at once.

## Export

Only the Safeguarding Lead has **Export to CSV**, and it exports the
summary columns of the current list — never contact details, health or
ID numbers. Every export is recorded as a critical event with the row
count. If you need a list for a funder, ask the Lead and say why.

## If something is wrong

A case you cannot open, a field you cannot see, a button that is not
there: that is the access rule working, not a fault. Ask the Safeguarding
Lead to reassign the case to you if it is yours to work. If you believe a
record is wrong and you cannot edit it, add a note saying so.
